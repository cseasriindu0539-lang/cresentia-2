Final Sequence

Enter all 10 characters to unlock victory.

Puzzle Hints

  1. From the launch screen: count unique “ERRORS” clusters on the left.
  2. Take the 2nd letter of Crescentia.
  3. Room number of the registration desk (last digit).
  4. Day of event (1=Mon, …, 7=Sun).
  5. The year Crescentia was founded (last digit).
  6. Number of organizers modulo 10.
  7. First letter of the host department.
  8. Total number of stalls (last digit).
  9. First digit of event start time (24h).
  10. Sum of QR posters near auditorium modulo 10.

Tip: Letters are case-insensitive. Use numbers or symbols if your clue demands it.
